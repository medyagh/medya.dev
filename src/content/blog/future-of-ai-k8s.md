# The Future of AI and Kubernetes

As we look towards the next decade of cloud-native infrastructure, the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes is becoming the most critical area of innovation.

With my work on Gemini Cloud Assist and bringing GPU workloads to local environments via Minikube, I've seen firsthand how AI can dramatically lower the barrier to entry for complex system management while simultaneously requiring robust, scalable orchestration.

Stay tuned for more thoughts on this topic!
